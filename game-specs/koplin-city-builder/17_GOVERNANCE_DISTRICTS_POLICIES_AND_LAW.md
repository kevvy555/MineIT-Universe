# 17 — Governance, Districts, Policies and Law

## Constitutional framing

The Commonwealth deliberately distributes authority. The city-builder must therefore model the player as a powerful steward operating through institutions, not as an unrestricted ruler.

KCB-GOV-001 — Every non-sandbox player action that materially affects rights/property SHOULD have an authority basis.

KCB-GOV-002 — Commonwealth-level constraints cannot normally be switched off by local policy.

## Governance layers

Abstract layers:
- Commonwealth/planetary: rights, high-risk AI, major standards, intercontinental/off-world coordination;
- metropolitan/city: land use, transport, local infrastructure, service budgets, local fiscal instruments;
- district/neighbourhood: local policies, public realm priorities, service emphasis.

KCB-GOV-010 — The game does not need to simulate a full parliament, but jurisdiction must explain why some options are available or unavailable.

## Mandate

Player political capital/mandate represents:
- public trust;
- institutional cooperation;
- statutory authority;
- capacity to undertake disruptive reform.

It is not simply popularity.

KCB-GOV-020 — Routine operational decisions do not consume mandate.
KCB-GOV-021 — Major compulsory acquisition, large tax shifts, heritage demolition or disruptive megaprojects MAY require mandate.
KCB-GOV-022 — Good outcomes can rebuild mandate over time.

## Consultation and approval

For major projects:
1. proposal;
2. feasibility;
3. public/institutional consultation abstractly;
4. revision;
5. approval;
6. procurement;
7. construction.

KCB-GOV-030 — The player may fast-track emergencies at higher fiscal/mandate cost.
KCB-GOV-031 — Consultation should be a strategic timing/trade-off mechanic, not repetitive dialog clicking.

## District policies

Policy categories:
- land use/density;
- transport/access;
- parking/curb;
- freight timing;
- environmental;
- building efficiency;
- heritage;
- housing affordability;
- public space;
- industry incentives;
- service prioritisation.

Each policy defines:
- jurisdiction;
- eligibility;
- implementation delay;
- operating cost;
- affected metrics;
- side effects;
- review/cooldown.

KCB-GOV-040 — Policies MUST have explicit causal effects rather than vague percent bonuses wherever possible.

## Policy stacking

KCB-GOV-050 — Conflicting policies require precedence rules.
KCB-GOV-051 — The UI MUST show the final effective rule for a parcel/building.
KCB-GOV-052 — Duplicate equivalent bonuses do not stack accidentally.

## Rights and protections

Game-level abstractions should preserve:
- equal legal personhood;
- no species discrimination;
- no AI sovereignty;
- property/acquisition process;
- basic service access;
- historical safeguards.

KCB-GOV-060 — The game MUST NOT offer species-targeted discriminatory policies as optimisation tools.

## Heritage law

Heritage designations:
- listed building;
- protected civic space;
- protected view/corridor optional;
- archaeological/historic site;
- cultural landscape.

KCB-GOV-070 — Heritage protection constrains demolition/redevelopment but can provide identity, tourism/cultural and civic value.

## Corporate power

Companies can be influential and commercially important but not sovereign.

KCB-GOV-080 — Companies cannot create private law, own citizens or replace the city government.
KCB-GOV-081 — Public-private contracts can create dependence, cost and lobbying/mandate pressure without granting sovereignty.

## Civic events and opinion

Public opinion can form around:
- housing;
- fares;
- taxes;
- demolition;
- service quality;
- historic memory;
- environmental change;
- major projects.

KCB-GOV-090 — Opinion SHOULD be issue-specific, not one permanent approval number.

## Elections / terms

Detailed elections are optional.

If enabled:
- elections assess mandate periodically;
- policy blocs/coalitions are issue-driven;
- losing mandate may constrain objectives or end a scenario, not delete the city.

KCB-GOV-100 — Political modelling MUST avoid mapping modern partisan labels directly onto Koplin without authored lore.

## Budget governance

KCB-GOV-110 — Budget changes can have implementation delays; firing/hiring, procurement and contracts are not instantaneous.
KCB-GOV-111 — Multi-year capital plans SHOULD show committed future expenditure.

## Governance UI

Required:
- authority/jurisdiction;
- active policies;
- policy conflicts;
- mandate;
- project approval pipeline;
- district metrics;
- consultation/issues;
- legal/heritage constraints.

KCB-GOV-120 — Disabled actions explain the legal/institutional reason and route to unlock/approve if possible.

## Acceptance criteria

- Protected building cannot be bulldozed by ordinary tool.
- Policy applies only to its district and updates effective rules.
- Large project can be delayed by insufficient mandate without arbitrary random rejection.
- Species-targeted discriminatory policy does not exist.
- Corporate partner can influence project economics but cannot override law.
