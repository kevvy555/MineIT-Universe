# 18 — Environment, Climate, Ecology and Pollution

## Canon context

Concordia sits in a cool-to-warm temperate, well-watered metropolitan plain on the Zoran continent. Exact climate values are not yet canonical and must be authored as game data until approved.

## Environmental layers

- air quality;
- water quality;
- noise;
- heat;
- soil/ground contamination;
- habitat/green connectivity;
- stormwater/flood risk;
- visual/light nuisance optional;
- carbon/planetary impact optional.

KCB-ENV-001 — Environmental effects MUST be spatial where location matters.

KCB-ENV-002 — Advanced technology reduces but does not abolish externalities.

## Weather

Weather states:
- clear;
- cloud;
- rain;
- storm;
- heat/cold episodes;
- seasonal variations.

KCB-ENV-010 — Weather affects visuals, sound and at least selected simulation systems.
KCB-ENV-011 — Rainfall affects drainage/flooding.
KCB-ENV-012 — Temperature may affect energy, outdoor activity and vegetation.

## Seasons

KCB-ENV-020 — Seasonal presentation SHOULD alter vegetation, daylight/activity and climate demand without making every season a repetitive crisis.

KCB-ENV-021 — Seasonal system must fit Koplin 3 astronomy once those parameters are canonically fixed; until then use game-only values.

## Air quality

Sources:
- industry;
- traffic where propulsion emits relevant pollutants;
- construction;
- incidents.

Sinks/mitigations:
- clean technology;
- filtration;
- distance;
- wind dispersion abstraction;
- green infrastructure limited effect.

KCB-ENV-030 — Pollution dispersion SHOULD use a performant field/grid model rather than individual particles.

## Noise

Sources:
- major roads;
- freight;
- transit;
- industry;
- construction;
- nightlife/events.

KCB-ENV-040 — Building insulation and barriers reduce indoor impact.
KCB-ENV-041 — Noise has time-of-day profiles.

## Heat

Urban heat depends on:
- hard surfaces;
- building density;
- waste heat;
- vegetation;
- water;
- shade.

KCB-ENV-050 — Green/water interventions can reduce local heat but must not act as magic universal bonuses.

## Water ecology

KCB-ENV-060 — Untreated/overloaded discharge degrades receiving water.
KCB-ENV-061 — Wetland/river restoration can improve ecology and public realm while consuming developable land.

## Habitat

KCB-ENV-070 — Habitat is represented as connected patches/corridors rather than only total green percentage.
KCB-ENV-071 — New roads can fragment habitat.
KCB-ENV-072 — Wildlife itself may remain mostly decorative/aggregated unless a scenario needs detailed ecology.

## Resource/land use

The capital region may contain peri-urban farms, woodlands, reservoirs and research/agricultural belts according to the atlas gradient.

KCB-ENV-080 — Urban expansion into high-value ecology/agriculture creates explicit opportunity cost.

## Environmental policy

Tools:
- protection areas;
- emissions standards;
- clean-technology incentives;
- low-emission/freight rules;
- noise limits;
- stormwater requirements;
- green-roof/permeability rules;
- restoration projects.

KCB-ENV-090 — Environmental rules can increase construction/operating cost while improving long-term conditions.

## Monitoring

Sensors/monitoring create detailed information.

KCB-ENV-100 — Core environmental data is available at strategic resolution by default; advanced monitoring may improve precision/prediction rather than hide basic gameplay information.

## Visual communication

Environmental state should affect:
- haze;
- water appearance;
- vegetation;
- heat shimmer optional;
- street activity;
- maintenance/cleanup presence.

KCB-ENV-110 — Visual effects must be subtle enough that a clean advanced city does not look filthy merely because a metric is slightly below ideal.

## Metrics

- air quality exposure;
- noise exposure;
- heat exposure;
- water quality;
- habitat connectivity;
- permeable surface ratio;
- flood risk;
- protected land;
- environmental inequality by district.

KCB-ENV-120 — Exposure, not only city-wide average pollution, matters for population outcomes.

## Acceptance criteria

- Dense hard-surface district can run hotter than green district.
- Added retention reduces peak flooding downstream.
- Cleaner industry reduces local emissions with cost trade-off.
- Habitat corridor loses connectivity when cut by development.
- Environmental overlays distinguish source intensity from population exposure.
