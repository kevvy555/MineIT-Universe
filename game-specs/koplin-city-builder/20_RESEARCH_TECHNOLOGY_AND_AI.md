# 20 — Research, Technology and AI

## Technology framing

The player governs a Year-5300 capital, not an early industrial town. Most foundational technology already exists. Progression therefore represents deployment, modernisation, specialisation, local pilot programmes and capital access rather than “discovering electricity.”

KCB-TECH-001 — Core urban services MUST not be locked behind implausible population milestones.

## Technology programme types

- infrastructure modernisation;
- clean energy;
- storage/grid control;
- water reuse;
- advanced transit;
- construction automation;
- building efficiency;
- medical/public-health;
- waste/material recovery;
- logistics;
- civic digital systems;
- bounded AI decision support;
- environmental restoration;
- advanced manufacturing;
- Veyrite-adjacent specialist programmes where appropriate.

## Research capacity

Research output depends on:
- institutions;
- qualified workforce;
- funding;
- specialised facilities;
- collaboration;
- data/compute;
- time.

KCB-TECH-010 — A research building without skilled staff does not generate full progress.
KCB-TECH-011 — Research progress may be shared city-wide but its inputs remain physical.

## Development tree philosophy

Technology programmes form branching investment trees.

KCB-TECH-020 — Branching SHOULD create sequencing choices, not permanently lock the player out of whole categories unless a scenario explicitly uses exclusive policy choices.

KCB-TECH-021 — Unlocking makes an option available; deployment still costs money, space, staff and maintenance.

## Existing advanced technology

Baseline technologies include:
- mature fusion;
- high-density storage;
- precision fabrication;
- advanced medicine;
- robotics;
- bounded AI;
- rapid planetary transport;
- advanced computation/networking.

KCB-TECH-030 — These systems must look and behave advanced from game start.

## Veyrite

Veyrite is strategic and rare and enables Vector Exchange interstellar technology.

For homeworld city play, Veyrite may affect:
- research institutes;
- specialist manufacturing;
- high-value economic demand;
- off-world logistics;
- prestige/strategic projects.

KCB-TECH-040 — Veyrite MUST NOT become generic magical city fuel.

## Bounded AI

Allowed civic AI functions:
- demand forecasting;
- traffic/transit scheduling;
- predictive maintenance;
- resource optimisation;
- anomaly detection;
- scenario modelling;
- routine dispatch;
- player recommendations.

Forbidden framing:
- autonomous sovereign mayor;
- unaccountable policy maker;
- self-expanding unrestricted system;
- AI making irreversible strategic decisions without authority.

KCB-TECH-050 — Every high-capability AI feature has a defined domain.
KCB-TECH-051 — Strategic recommendations identify responsible human/civic authority.
KCB-TECH-052 — Critical AI operations generate audit logs abstractly.
KCB-TECH-053 — Critical networks have fallback/degraded modes.

## AI assistance as UI

Optional player-assistance settings:
- explain bottlenecks;
- suggest transit changes;
- identify unused capacity;
- forecast policy effect;
- warn about unintended consequences.

KCB-TECH-060 — Assistance MUST not secretly perform irreversible player actions unless explicitly authorised as automation.

## Automation policies

The player can automate:
- maintenance scheduling;
- transit frequency within constraints;
- service mutual aid;
- utility dispatch;
- procurement reorder points.

KCB-TECH-070 — Automation operates inside player-set policies/bounds.
KCB-TECH-071 — Automation failure should be diagnosable and auditable.

## Technology obsolescence

Long-lived cities need change.

KCB-TECH-080 — Older systems may become less efficient or costly to maintain relative to new standards without becoming instantly nonfunctional.

KCB-TECH-081 — Retrofit competes with replacement.

## Pilot projects

KCB-TECH-090 — Advanced options may require a pilot district/facility before city-wide deployment.
KCB-TECH-091 — Pilot outcomes provide data and may reduce deployment cost/risk.

## Research UI

For each programme:
- prerequisites;
- required institutions/capacity;
- progress;
- ongoing cost;
- unlock outcome;
- deployment cost;
- operational trade-offs;
- lore/canon context.

KCB-TECH-100 — No research node is labelled only “+10% efficiency” without explaining the mechanism.

## Acceptance criteria

- Research output drops if specialist labour is unavailable.
- Unlocking a new transit technology does not instantly build it.
- AI can propose a route change but cannot approve major policy itself.
- Critical service can fall back when AI/communications are unavailable.
- Retrofit and replacement offer different cost/disruption profiles.
