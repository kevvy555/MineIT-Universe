# 26 — Events, News, Narrative and Living World

## Philosophy

Narrative should emerge from the city rather than sit above it as random popups.

KCB-EVT-001 — Events SHOULD begin from simulation conditions, authored calendar/canon or clearly identified scenario scripting.

## Event categories

- civic;
- cultural;
- economic;
- infrastructure;
- research;
- political/consultation;
- environmental;
- transport;
- institutional;
- historical commemoration;
- external Commonwealth/off-world news.

## Event structure

Each event contains:
- trigger;
- prerequisites;
- location;
- participants/affected systems;
- start;
- duration;
- consequences;
- player options if any;
- follow-up;
- news presentation;
- history record.

KCB-EVT-010 — Consequences should flow through ordinary systems wherever possible.

Example: a major festival increases trips and service demand rather than applying a direct “-10 traffic” penalty.

## City calendar

Recurring:
- civic commemorations;
- cultural festivals;
- academic terms;
- planned maintenance;
- budget year;
- major market/convention events.

KCB-EVT-020 — Calendar events are visible in advance when institutions would realistically know them.

## Dynamic events

Triggers can include:
- new population threshold;
- severe housing burden;
- landmark completion;
- service success/failure;
- company expansion;
- research milestone;
- district regeneration;
- heritage controversy;
- sustained congestion;
- external market shift.

KCB-EVT-030 — Randomness chooses among eligible events but does not ignore prerequisites.

## News system

News items answer:
- what happened;
- where;
- who is affected;
- why it matters;
- metric change;
- link to map/panel.

KCB-EVT-040 — News can be flavourful but cannot lie about core simulation facts.

## Perspectives

Different institutional/media perspectives may frame the same event differently:
- civic administration;
- independent media;
- local district;
- business;
- cultural institution.

KCB-EVT-050 — Perspective changes emphasis, not objective underlying values.

## Named history

The city history log stores:
- major project openings;
- district formation/rename;
- major incidents;
- historic demographic/economic milestones;
- heritage designation;
- notable citizen/institution events.

KCB-EVT-060 — History survives save upgrades.

## Emergent stories

Potential templates:
- longest-running business;
- household generations in one district;
- transit line milestone;
- station serving a major event;
- building saved from demolition;
- district transformed by project.

KCB-EVT-070 — Story selection reads existing simulation history instead of inventing incompatible facts.

## External events

The wider Commonwealth can influence:
- migration;
- trade;
- strategic investment;
- visitors;
- research demand.

KCB-EVT-080 — External events operate through explicit connector parameters.

## Player choices

Event decisions should be used sparingly.

KCB-EVT-090 — Major choice UI must state likely trade-offs and authority/cost.
KCB-EVT-091 — “Correct” answer should rarely be obvious stat maximisation.

## Notification load

KCB-EVT-100 — Routine events aggregate into city news feed.
KCB-EVT-101 — Only time-sensitive player decisions interrupt normal play.
KCB-EVT-102 — Narrative channels are filterable.

## Acceptance criteria

- Festival event creates trips through normal transport simulation.
- Historic building story references its real construction/renovation history.
- External market event changes import/export parameters, not hidden production output.
- News item can focus camera on affected district.
- Repeated event types do not fire every day under unchanged conditions due to cooldowns.
