# 19 — Culture, History, Identity and Social Systems

## Purpose

Koplin’s history is one of the strongest differentiators of the game. Culture must appear in the city through institutions, public space, architecture, festivals, memory and household life without turning the three peoples into game classes.

## Identity model

A citizen may carry:
- ancestry mix;
- cultural affiliations;
- ancestral language familiarity optional;
- district/community affiliation;
- civic identity.

KCB-CULT-001 — Identity attributes MUST NOT grant broad productivity/intelligence/combat modifiers.

KCB-CULT-002 — Mixed ancestry is ordinary and can include multiple cultural affiliations.

## Cultural traditions

Trondonian historical themes:
- stewardship;
- biological knowledge;
- food/water systems;
- reciprocal obligation.

Zoran historical themes:
- technical institutions;
- surveying;
- engineering standards;
- exploration;
- city-state/guild legacy.

Blaxmar historical themes:
- communal survival;
- thermal/energy stewardship;
- logistics;
- Dark Life;
- resistance/history of occupation.

KCB-CULT-010 — These themes MAY influence architecture, institutions, festivals and district history, but cannot become deterministic job bonuses.

## Dark Life

Dark Life persists as faith and cultural heritage focused on stored ancient life, shared heat, mutual aid and stewardship.

KCB-CULT-020 — Dark Life sites/events may appear as cultural/religious institutions.
KCB-CULT-021 — Representation should reflect its canon as more than literal coal worship.
KCB-CULT-022 — Modern participation may include non-Blaxmars.

## Historical memory

Key commemorative themes:
- first contacts;
- conquest/occupation;
- reform/reparations;
- global technical institutions;
- AI Wars;
- Commonwealth Compact;
- Veyrite breakthrough;
- space/interstellar age.

KCB-CULT-030 — Memorials and museums should preserve uncomfortable history rather than sanitise it.

## District identity

District identity accumulates from:
- age;
- dominant building forms;
- institutions;
- public spaces;
- heritage;
- resident history;
- cultural venues;
- economic role;
- major events.

KCB-CULT-040 — Identity changes slowly and has memory.
KCB-CULT-041 — A district does not become “arts district” instantly because one gallery is placed.

## Culture and leisure demand

Citizens generate trips for:
- parks;
- museums;
- performance;
- food/markets;
- sport/recreation;
- festivals;
- religious/cultural gathering;
- waterfront/greenway activity.

KCB-CULT-050 — Leisure demand varies with age, household and schedule.

## Events and festivals

KCB-CULT-060 — Festivals occupy real venues and create temporary travel/service demand.
KCB-CULT-061 — Events can strengthen district identity and economic activity.
KCB-CULT-062 — Large events require crowd/transport planning.

## Heritage

Buildings/sites can gain significance through:
- canonical status;
- age;
- unique architecture;
- major event association;
- civic role.

KCB-CULT-070 — Player history can create future heritage: an ordinary station may become historically significant after centuries.

## Social cohesion

Do not use one “ethnic tension” meter.

Relevant social pressures:
- inequality;
- displacement;
- service disparity;
- historical controversy;
- civic trust;
- isolation;
- rapid demographic change;
- project disruption.

KCB-CULT-080 — Social tension events should be issue-driven.

## Public art and naming

KCB-CULT-090 — Player may name roads, parks, stations and districts.
KCB-CULT-091 — Public-art projects can improve place identity/culture but should not act as universal happiness spam.

## Media

The Universe contains media organisations such as Farline News Network in structured expansion data.

KCB-CULT-100 — News may report city outcomes from different issue perspectives without fabricating partisan ideology.
KCB-CULT-101 — Media reports should help surface consequences and public interpretation, not merely flavour text.

## Tourism/visitation

Concordia as federal capital attracts visitors for government, culture, research and major events.

KCB-CULT-110 — Visitors use real transport/service capacity and spend in relevant sectors.
KCB-CULT-111 — Tourism is not the city’s only prestige mechanism.

## Acceptance criteria

- Mixed-cultural district can host multiple traditions without segregation rules.
- Heritage identity persists through ordinary redevelopment.
- Festival causes measurable transit/crowd demand.
- Historical controversy can create a civic issue without species stereotyping.
- A long-lived player-created landmark can become eligible for heritage status.
