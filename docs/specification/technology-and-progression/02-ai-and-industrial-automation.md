# UNI-TECH-002 — AI, Robotics and the Automation Trade-off

**Owner:** MineIT-Universe shared design · **Status:** canonical Year-5326 restrictions, common industrial-design interpretation · **Source:** [Deep Reach §28 AI, Automation and Robotics](../../../data/lore/Koplin_Scenario_II_Deep_Reach_Mining_Charter.md), [AI Wars / Commonwealth background](../../../data/lore/Koplin_Universe_Expanded_Backstory_Lore_Bible.md).

## Fictional and legal context

The Koplin Commonwealth retains constraints on autonomous AI following the historic AI wars. At the Year-5326 Deep Reach era, **bounded industrial assistance is common**: analysing samples, optimising haul routes, maintaining warehouse movement, scheduling maintenance, modelling ore deposits, predicting equipment failures and supporting medical diagnosis.

The lore restricts systems from independently:
- changing settlement strategy or claiming worlds;
- entering new financial contracts;
- concealing production;
- commanding unrestricted security operations;
- replicating into uncontrolled devices;
- removing biological oversight from life-critical functions.

These are **canon social/regulatory constraints**, not proof that every platform must simulate individual AI regulation hearings or control agents.

## A real industrial decision, not only productivity progression

Automation may reduce worker-hour demand but increase:
- sensor, electronics, computing and actuator installation;
- precision calibration and stable Power;
- ongoing specialist replacement Parts;
- imported high-grade components where local precision manufacturing is immature;
- environmental monitoring and safe human override.

Conversely a less automated mine can need more workers while requiring simpler locally serviceable Parts. The economic choice includes wages/crew logistics, capital cost, spare stocks, reliability and remote travel. An automated asteroid mine still requires physical extraction tools and true depletion accounting.

## Shared Parts/Machinery economy impact

**Control/Computing/Communications** machinery and **Electrical/Computing Parts** are proposed Mobile groupings, not extra authored Universe canon IDs. Desktop can keep physically distinct sensors/actuators/processors. Automated shipyards, orbital stations and gas harvesters draw compatible electrical/control capital and maintenance inputs from the **same industrial economy**, subject to the higher-rated environment and fail-safe constraints.

## Cross-platform implementation boundary

- Desktop: possible per-machine automation quality, skill substitution, maintenance/calibration and command rule.
- Mobile: aggregated staff reduction versus operating spare/Power demand, automated procurement/maintenance policies and critical manual controls.
- Universe: authored identities and narrative constraints, not the exact workforce efficiency multiplier.

Do not introduce military technology or unrestricted self-modifying AI to 'complete' an industrial capability taxonomy. Those are outside the current mining corporation's accepted scope.

**Open:** which exact worker roles can be automated, AI-law enforcement gameplay depth, capability tier identifiers, emergency human-oversight rule, and actual savings/maintenance balance.
