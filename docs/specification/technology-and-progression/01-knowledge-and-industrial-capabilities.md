# UNI-TECH-001 — Technology, Knowledge and Capability Deployment

**Owner:** shared MineIT design · **Status:** canonical lore + proposed common principles, not one platform's technology tree · **Sources:** [Crash scenario technological reconstruction](../../../data/lore/Koplin_Universe_Expanded_Backstory_Lore_Bible.md), [Deep Reach §20 and §32](../../../data/lore/Koplin_Scenario_II_Deep_Reach_Mining_Charter.md), [Desktop research designs](https://github.com/kevvy555/MineIT-Desktop/blob/main/Documentation/design/Research/ResearchTechnologies.md), [Mobile engineering plans](https://github.com/kevvy555/MineIT-Android/blob/develop/docs/migration/PHASE_7_7_TECHNOLOGY_ENGINEERING.md).

## Knowledge is not finished equipment

A civilisation knowing how an induction motor or Vector Exchange Drive works is not the same as a particular colony having suitable **refined stock, calibration, workshop capability, skilled people, Power and assembly time**. A blueprint can exist without suitable raw materials; a shipment of Machinery can be installed locally without the colony itself manufacturing the machine.

Three different constraints:
1. **Scientific/engineering knowledge:** research, analysis and validated techniques; may be corporation-wide or expedition-held.
2. **Industrial/technical capability:** available tools, laboratories, refiners, tolerances, manufacturing methods and authorised safety/environment standard.
3. **Physically deployed capital:** installed machines, compatible materials/consumables, staffed facilities, inventory at the actual operating asset.

Do not replace all three with one universal 'tech level' unlocking arbitrary conversion of raw Ore into a ship.

## Cross-game progression meaning

**Desktop crash reconstruction:** salvage/survive -> local compatible materials -> industrial base -> precision manufacturing -> orbital capability -> interstellar/anomaly reconstruction. Research largely means **adaptation and validation against the crash-world's unknown materials**, not rediscovery of all modern science.

**Mobile corporation:** acquire knowledge and site engineering capability commercially, trade imported equipment, then expand into local refining, Parts manufacture and Machine assembly, eventually industrial hubs and shared ship/station production. Starting equipment imports are legitimate even where the colony lacks the ability to manufacture them.

Industrial depth and physical suitable raw feedstock combine; neither guarantees a particular facility can run alone.

## Compatibility and category progression

A functional Machinery family may have increasing **capability/environmental grades** when justified by technology. A low-grade rover propulsion engine is not a Veyrite drive merely because both are 'Mobility and Propulsion'. Compatibility must remain physical and explicit; proposed grading is **not yet approved** for Mobile's inventory IDs [D00](https://github.com/kevvy555/MineIT-Android/issues/114).

Research/technology categories should preserve distinctions between survey science, geological extraction method, Power generation, Food/biology, refining, manufacture, automation, communications, navigation/propulsion, life-support and safety. These may be displayed very differently by platform.

## Engineering constraints for future writers

- Shared lore defines what is *possible* and the authored equipment identities, not the exact number of research points.
- Each game owns tree nodes, prerequisites, pacing, productivity and UI.
- A researched machine doesn't teleport to a remote outpost; it still needs purchased/manufactured and transported equipment.
- A new machine requiring unusual high-tech parts should consume physically plausible refined inputs, not make previous generic machinery stock valueless without a compatibility reason.
- Desktop may expose individual calibration/material research; Mobile may treat it as a ranked capability purchase.
- Persisted game technology should be versioned without contaminating canonical Universe authored data.

## Open decisions

Whether common authored technology IDs should exist in Universe, how later craft equipment ratings and scarce Veyrite components become standardised, and any neutral cross-game interoperability schema remain open. Do **not** publish a fake common global tech tree merely to fill a specification file.
