# UNI-WORLD-001 — Worlds, Celestial Bodies and Physical Truth

**Authority:** [World Surface Canon](../../WorldSurfaceCanon.md), [world lore](../../../data/lore/Koplin_Universe_World_Surface.md), [foundation lore](../../../data/lore/Koplin_Universe_Expanded_Backstory_Lore_Bible.md). **Status:** shared conceptual specification; do not copy gameplay-specific algorithms into canon.

## What the Universe owns

- Galaxy/system/world authored identities and relationships, including known Koplin Reach systems and named worlds.
- Celestial-body classifications and environmental vocabulary: planets, moons, asteroids, rings and gas giants have different physical surface/atmosphere possibilities.
- Substance physical identities, properties and rules restricting where different types could plausibly occur.
- Canonical lore geography, historical events and the limits of Year-5326 technology.

## What a game creates

A game's deterministic world generator can produce additional **save-owned** systems, planet layouts, regions, reserves and deposits under those shared physical constraints. Generated map chunks, scan progress, ore stock remaining, company claims and colony sites are not independent authored Universe canon.

Desktop may generate a detailed navigable 3D landscape around individual sampling coordinates. Mobile may materialise a broader regional frontier and compact colony surface. These **differing generators are allowed** provided their outputs respect common body kinds and physical meanings.

## Environment and asset placement

- **Gas giant:** an atmosphere envelope, **not** a normal permanent ground/forest tile grid. Harvesting requires appropriate mobile atmospheric, orbital or floating infrastructure.
- **Asteroid:** a small body with its own geometry and deposits, not simply a downsized forested planet.
- **Airless/cold moon:** can permit extraction and sealed orbital/ground facilities, but living systems require suitable power and life support.
- **Rocky biological world:** geological and native living-resource truth coexist, but managed agriculture and player-made settlements are mutable state.

## Canon vs player discovery

The player and Deep Reach may have incomplete/conflicting survey information about a real world. Their knowledge view **does not create resource occurrences**. Different game scale and scanner systems can reveal compatible shared physical facts without matching UI or sample-resolution algorithms.

## Missing shared details

Deeper atmosphere/pressure/ring/comet taxonomy and exact resource compatibility need reconciliation with the full source catalogue and Desktop generation designs. No fixed Mobile first-pass 64-system cap belongs in this shared chapter.
