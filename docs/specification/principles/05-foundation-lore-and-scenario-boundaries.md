## Persistent chronology and protected history

The [foundation](../../../data/lore/Koplin_Universe_Expanded_Backstory_Lore_Bible.md) establishes: Koplin 3 is the roughly half-land/half-water temperate home of closely related Trondonians, Zorans and Blaxmars from common ancestry; resource/geographical divergence explains early biological, agrarian/metallurgical/coal-fuel industrial history without essentialist species stereotypes. The Blaxmar war/conquest ~Years 700–800 led to centuries of reparations and constitutional reconciliation, not eternal species hostility. Three historical languages contribute to modern **Common Koplin Standard**; Dark Life survives as a cultural/ethical tradition around shared heat and stewardship. Year 0 is retrospectively anchored to a recorded **double-moon conjunction**.

AI Wars around **Year 2400** were a failure of institutions delegating strategic autonomy to competing systems; the **Year-2600 Commonwealth Compact** defines no sovereign AI, accountable biological decision-makers, bounded purpose, audit, segmentation and emergency fallback. Automated logistics, bounded assistance, scientific diagnosis and machine control are possible **only within that governance**; self-directed strategic AI takeover, unrestricted security and automatic corporate contract execution would contradict canon.

Veyrite, discovered **Year 5100**, is a strategic rare phase-coherent **lattice** enabling the **Vector Exchange** (VX) momentum mechanism. It is **not** routine crystalline ore, a consumable fuel itself, an instant jump/warp/wormhole, or unlimited propulsion near massive gravitating bodies. Mature transit is of order **four weeks per light-year** (historical lore baseline, not a requirement for game clock balance), with navigational waypoints, gravity-well disengagement, imperfect delayed communications and ageing lattice/cooling/reactor-fuel demands. Veyrite lattice wear/annealing differs from **manufactured Fusion Fuel and local Propellant** consumables. Common technology is advanced by ~Year 5300 but requires maintained industrial supply chains, precision manufacturing, calibration and Power — knowledge alone does not create equipment.

Foundation lore is **higher than** named entities and products under current `data/`; do not copy long narrative, character rosters or Appendix A 38 historical continuity decisions into platform mechanics. Canon designer truth can be visible to the Directory/Lore Explorer while not available to game characters.

Water and native Food require **analysis before potability/edibility**; visual scenery never proves material identity. Emergency Captain/Mission Director authority transitions toward an elected Settlement Council (foundation suggests ~90 days). Four survivor outlooks (Homebound, Founders, Seekers and Stewards) are **cross-species/social objectives**, not fixed racial workforce or AI factions. The artificial **Anchor Network** is **designer-only hidden truth**: phase-coherent drive capture, limited anchor regions, recharge and field-energy constraints; **do not surface its existence to an opening player**, and never treat it as arbitrary teleportation.

## Year-5326 Deep Reach contractual and legal scenario facts

The full [original charter scenario](../../../data/lore/Koplin_Scenario_II_Deep_Reach_Mining_Charter.md) defines the setting's organisational model: Koplin Deep Reach Corporation is a powerful but **non-sovereign** mining/logistics/industry conglomerate with Prospecting, Extraction, Logistics, Industrial, Biochem, Frontier Services and Strategic Materials functions. Commonwealth citizen rights, courts, life-safety and environmental law outrank any company agreement. Deep Reach cannot legally own its employees, imprison them or wage private claim wars. Competition is mostly commercial/licensing/claims/standards/politics.

**Original Scenario II charter terms (narrative/source-era, not all current Mobile objective formulas):**
- Ten **standard years** of exclusive **first-offer external sale** of contract-qualifying raw or processed material from the registered claim; **local construction, water, Food, life support, maintenance, fuel, research, industrial and safety reserves are explicitly retained** by settlement. A contract is *not* a duty to sell the whole inventory or starve workers. Current Mobile stock channels and buyer servicing have more recent explicit game decisions.
- One-year starter hardware and **planned-use maintenance/resupply guarantee concurrent with year one**, not one extra contract year; irresponsible wear/expansion/catastrophic loss exhausts it early. This is the story of supply-chain dependency and ongoing machinery/Parts usage, **not** a mandatory existing Mobile HUD term/industrial victory condition.
- Deep Reach owns original/fixed **Charter Assets** through the term; expedition has contractual **operating control**; standard expiry transfers **direct operating possession** back to Deep Reach, not legal title which it already held. Pre-owned cooperative ship/private purchases are separate. Renewals, joint venture, asset buyout and new claim are **negotiated options**, not guaranteed rights. Individuals retain a right to leave; expedition abandonment can incur commercial penalties but not imprisonment.
- The campaign's capital ship **Wayfarer** and Standard Frontier Extraction Package-Class 500 contain modern habitat, fusion, mines, analyzers, field vehicles, processing/fabrication and spares. Source Appendix B offers a design-level count (four excavators, six loaders, etc.) **not a canonical inventory JSON SKU/count guarantee**; it does not make Mobile's charter reward a fully loaded starter ship. The recent Mobile **Pioneer charter award and separate paid loadout** have explicit later user-approval and must **not** be overwritten by this source.
- Collection/supply vessels are **real scheduled freight**, not a global instantaneous market. The original route table says roughly 60–90 days inner, 90–120 outer Koplin, 150–210 nearby interstellar and longer frontier custom. **These are Source II scenario narrative scales**, not Android's recent five-day corporate trade visit balance or exact buyer collection terms. Pricing examples and quality grades **D/C/B/A/S**, dynamic demand, proposed AAA–E rating, and one-year support/capital-labour economic equations are **scenario design motifs** until separately accepted per game.
- Year-one local sustainment, Year-two import support cliff, Years 3–8 refining/diversification and Year-nine/ten expiry planning are **narrative pacing**, not replacement for the later [approved Android charter resource mandate, reward and renewal calculations](https://github.com/kevvy555/MineIT-Android/blob/docs/industrial-design-spec-114/docs/specification/commerce-and-contracts/02-charters-and-claims.md).
- Scenario science starts with modern knowledge, practical unknown *local* materials. Scanning provides uncertainty; real surveying and material/biological tests precede confident extraction, industrial use and consumption. Natural or alien biological harvesting requires sustainability and environmental compliance. No secret guarantee that every world has ideal fuels, Ore and Food beside first colony; poor claims can require relocation, purchase and strategic abandonment.
