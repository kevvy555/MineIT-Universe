# UNI-PRIN-001 — Shared MineIT Vision and Design Boundaries

**Status:** shared design consolidation, partially agreed; canonical setting remains [lore hierarchy](../../CanonSourceHierarchy.md). **Owner:** MineIT-Universe shared design. **Sources:** [foundation backstory](../../../data/lore/Koplin_Universe_Expanded_Backstory_Lore_Bible.md), [Deep Reach Year-5326 scenario](../../../data/lore/Koplin_Scenario_II_Deep_Reach_Mining_Charter.md), [Desktop concept](https://github.com/kevvy555/MineIT-Desktop/blob/main/Documentation/vision-brief.md), [Mobile industrial discovery](https://github.com/kevvy555/MineIT-Android/issues/114).

## Common fiction, multiple game experiences

MineIT games inhabit the same published universe and material laws. A particular colony or ship can change over a save, but its physical environment, chemical constraints, civilisation and authored identities must remain consistent.

- **Desktop** explores survival after an expedition is stranded in an unfamiliar universe: sample/local discovery, machinery, deep 3D exploration, long-term colony development and escape.
- **Mobile** explores a Year-5326 Deep Reach mining corporation: expeditions to claims, extraction, trade and fleet management, and future interstellar industrial independence.
- A game can implement a subset of industrial capabilities or abstract many physical machines into functional kits **without rewriting universal physical facts**.

## Universally useful principles

1. **Discover rather than manifest:** generated physical resources have seeded identity, location and state independent of whether the player knows them.
2. **Substance plausibility:** a rock, fuel, biological resource or refined metal is not interchangeable with another merely because the UI uses the same commodity group.
3. **Technology requires materials:** fabricating complex machinery depends on appropriate feedstock, processes and calibration, not just a universal 'Ore' counter.
4. **Progression emerges from capability:** survey -> extraction -> usable feedstock -> manufactured equipment -> maintenance and expansion, at platform-specific detail.
5. **Survival is contextual:** Food, Air, Water, climate, Power and infrastructure vary by world and asset location.
6. **Logistics is physical:** inventory belongs somewhere; cargo transport, distance and time influence self-sufficiency.
7. **One industrial economy:** planets, fleets, stations, orbit and atmospheric assets share compatible Parts/Machinery and materials; platform-specific operations differ.
8. **Agency and consequence:** export now or process later; extract aggressively or sustainably; use scarce imports or invest in local production.

## Never universalise platform game rules

Desktop's hourly tick, crash-start people counts, launch-readiness pacing and 3D placement are **Desktop rules**. Mobile's 64-system first-pass seed, Food/Build/Fuel/Ore trade projections, HQ limits, charter quota and aggregated day progression are **Mobile rules**. They are neither lore mandates nor automatic requirements of future games.
