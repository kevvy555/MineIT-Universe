# UNI-MAT-001 — Raw, Refined and Manufactured Material Identity

**Authority:** [Materials companion](../../../data/lore/Koplin_Universe_Materials_And_Substances.md), [Substance Canon](../../SubstanceCanon.md), [Universe substance records](../../../data/substances.json); **Status:** canonical identity plus cross-game design interpretation.

## Physical categories

- **Raw resources** are naturally occurring deposits, gaseous/liquid constituents and biological materials.
- **Refined or processed materials** have undergone a real transformation and have properties that can make them useful for infrastructure, chemistry or high-precision machinery.
- **Manufactured components (Parts)** consume applicable refined inputs, process, tooling, energy and work.
- **Functional Machines** are manufactured/assembled from appropriate Parts and other physical stock, then installed into Assets.
- **Operating consumables** (Food, Fuels, Propellants, lubricants and certain spares) disappear or change in stock according to use.

The categories describe different physical economic roles, not automatically the same warehouse UI grouping. Example: a refinery can output conductive stock, but a mine's unrefined Copper Ore is not a finished controller.

## Examples (material relationships, not formal recipes)

| Feedstock | Refined class | Typical applicability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Structural metal ores | Structural alloys | Shell, beams, mechanical Parts, frames |
| Conductive ores/silica | Conductive stock, glass and electrical insulation | Computers, motors, Power systems |
| Clay and heat-resistant silicates | Ceramic/refractory stock | Thermal components, high-temp processing |
| Carbon/oil/gas/biomass | Graphite, lubricant, polymer and chemical feedstock | Seals, bearings, filters, processing |
| Lithium/Cobalt/rare earths | Battery/magnetic/electrical stock | Storage, sensors, high-tech systems |
| Appropriate hydrogen/helium | Separately specified compatible gases and fuel products | Power, propulsion, industrial processing |

Material processing is **not** a free generic upgrade: actual techniques, yields, impurities, quality and byproducts belong in a game's recipe/technology balance using canonical source materials.

## Cross-game projections

Desktop may store physical material instances with quality/thermal/reactivity properties and a precise component recipe. Mobile may group minerals as Food/Build/Fuel/Ore for navigation/trading today and propose simplified Parts/Machinery product families later. Such UI/market families **are not** a source of authored substance identities. Any future product that is genuinely a distinct physical object requires deliberate canon authoring.

## Future compatibility

No raw-equals-refined magical substitution, no universal 'Ore -> Industry -> Anything' rule. A capital machine is not daily consumed; genuine replacement Parts or consumables are expended. Ships and stations inherit the same constraints as planetary buildings.
