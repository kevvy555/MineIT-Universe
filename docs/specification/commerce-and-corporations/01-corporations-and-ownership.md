# UNI-COM-001 — Corporation, Trade and Ownership Semantics

**Authority:** [Deep Reach scenario](../../../data/lore/Koplin_Scenario_II_Deep_Reach_Mining_Charter.md), [Commercial Buyer Model](../../CommercialBuyerModel.md), Universe organisation/operation data; **Status:** scenario canon plus shared rules.

## Commercial universe

Mining corporation roles include claim underwriting, extraction, processing, bulk freight, engineering, parts supply, manufactured machine supply, shipbuilding, research and specialised environmental services. Named actors, contact people and their organisational affiliation are authored Universe identities. **'Buyer' is a gameplay role/view**, not a parallel canonical entity database.

A corporation may own ships, employed personnel, site leases, inventory at different locations, production infrastructure, depots, cash balances, obligations and contracts. Ownership or stock movement in a player's save is mutable game state, not historical canon rewrites.

## Raw, process, retain

The charter scenario explicitly frames a resource's three choices: sell raw, refine for value or keep for internal construction/manufacture. Interstellar travel makes distance and cargo capacity strategically important; a low-value bulky refined construction material may be uneconomical to import into a remote system.

## Procure and deliver

Corporate collection/delivery visits provide both inbound orders (Food, spares, Machinery, construction supplies) and outbound mined/commercial cargo. Contract/financial identity remains tied to the real customer/corporation, but exact availability, price, visit timing, claim audit and local payment are defined by the game.

## Future distributed industry

Regional industrial hubs, repair/shipyard docks, fuel depots, orbit transfer stations, freighters and isolated mining colonies all participate in the same economic supply network. A company-owned warehouse stockpile at one world cannot magically equip a remote mine without a delivery route; each game chooses how to model cargo and scheduling.

## Game-specifics not canonicalised here

Mobile Deep Reach charter quotas, buyer reputation ladders and corporation UI are product rules. Desktop's survival/crash economics may rely on salvage rather than a viable corporation market. Share economic meanings, not exact reputation formula or prices.
